How this works

Black and white pixels are promises the code keeps; the rest is noise it fills however it likes. A solver pins your pixels exactly into the code's spare bits, then spends a little of its Reed-Solomon error budget on any it can't place — those show red, and the meter shows the spare budget left. The code always scans; it just stops honoring pixels it has no room for.

Any leftover freedom is pure noise: Randomize samples a fresh pattern with your art untouched. Paint less and there's more freedom; paint more and it shrinks toward one forced answer.
