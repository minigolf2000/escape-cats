# Piece 10 — wallpaper (repeating icon patterns at v10, 57x57) — build report

Generated by `node build-10-wallpaper.mjs` (deterministic; re-run to regenerate).

URL `https://github.com/minigolf2000/cat-games`, **v10-L (57x57)**, urlCase "schemehost", verified with
`allowSchemeHostCase` (jsQR @ scale 8/3/2). FIRST v10 piece.

**v10-L layout:** 4 blocks `[2x(86,68), 2x(87,69)]`, capacity 9 codewords/block.
The 41-char URL dirties only block 0 (72 frozen modules); blocks 1-3 are pure
padding. Block-aware **safe mask recomputed for v10**: a field cell is safe to
pin white iff its codeword is EC or in any block != 0 (local `safeMaskV10`; for
2-block v6 this is identical to the lib's `block===1`, so the shared lib is
**untouched**). Furniture: alignment patterns at every combo of {6,28,50}
including (28,28) dead center — every placement disqualified if any icon cell
lands on a function module (build-09 `heroFuncHits` gate).

**Intact-or-absent rule:** each icon INSTANCE ends COMPLETE (100% of its cells
dark + its required halo clean) or ABSENT (dropped whole before the final solve,
its cell surrendered to the white field). The solver probe-solves, measures per-
instance completeness, drops the single worst imperfect instance WHOLE, re-solves,
and repeats until every survivor is complete with headroom >=2 — searching 8 masks
x flip restarts and keeping the config with the most survivors, then sweeping
noise seeds for the whitest field.

**Three enabling techniques (this piece):**
- *Furniture clearance.* An icon jammed against a fixed pattern (e.g. a dot on the
  (28,28) alignment corner) is over-constrained and can NEVER reach 100% at any
  mask. Placements are required to keep a 1-2 module margin from furniture so every
  attempt sits on free, rank-satisfiable ground.
- *Soft white zones (build-01 clean-disc, per instance).* A best-effort white band
  is pinned around each glyph at high priority for visual separation, but is NOT a
  completeness gate. This lets a thin glyph whose 1-module interior gaps aren't
  independently whiteable (the compact Cool S) still READ on white without being
  dropped for a stray halo speck.
- *Bounded field band + post-hoc heroes.* The flip budget is tight (28 codewords
  total); pinning the entire safe field white can starve icons of budget, so the
  denser designs cap field-white to a band around the icons. For the lattice
  design no site is pre-protected — the solver keeps whatever solves 100% and the
  4 most-central SURVIVORS are labelled heroes post-hoc (complete by construction).

Gates: scale 8/3/2 scans, headroom >=2 on all 4 blocks, whiteness >=66%, keep
>=60% of attempted instances, heroes complete.

## Notes (art-notes round 1)

- **First v10 piece.** The harness handles v10-L end-to-end (standard encode, art
  solve, verify at scale 8/3/2). Key layout finding: ALL 72 frozen URL modules sit
  in cols 48-56 (block 0 interleaves to the right edge), so the whole cols 9-47
  canvas is clean, free ground. Icons live there; the right ~9 columns are the
  code's own grain. This is why every design reads as wallpaper-on-the-left with a
  QR seam on the right — an inherent v10 property, not a solver miss.
- **What actually drives the drops.** With freeDim ~1773 the field is easy; the
  binding constraints are (a) furniture clearance — a glyph too close to a fixed
  pattern is unsatisfiable at every mask; and (b) the 28-codeword flip budget —
  pinning icons + halos + a full white field can tip a block below 2 headroom, so
  the worst/peripheral instance is dropped. Both are handled up front (clearance
  filter + bounded field band), leaving the honest intact-or-absent drops small.
- **smiley-dots** — staggered polka grid (odd rows offset half a pitch), pitch 11,
  ring r4, 2-module furniture clearance; every clean-canvas site attempted, the 4
  most-central SURVIVORS labelled heroes. A dot's 1-ring halo can be linearly
  unsatisfiable even with perfect strokes, so a couple sites drop as designed
  blanks — the polka reads with gaps, never with a broken face.
- **cool-s-wall** — ONE full-size build-09 S (protected, perfect) with brick-tiled
  compact S's (verticals 4 tall, SAME topology / parallel waist / open ends). The
  compact outlines are stroke-only for completeness with a 2-module SOFT white
  zone, so they read as distinct S's without dropping on an unwhiteable interior
  gap. v10's furniture density caps the clean tiling area, so the wall runs a few
  S's rather than a dozen.
- **doodle-page** — the taste piece: a large Cool S + two smileys (r5/r3) + a solid
  triangle pair + a flat-top hexagon outline + a chunky 5-point star, arranged as a
  balanced rosette around the (28,28) alignment 'sticker', nothing touching, each
  ringed by a soft white halo. Hero = the S + the large smiley. All six survive.
