GOOMBA GLIDER

a silly-bandz line rider · cat games party game
Goomba's spider plants are thirsty. Ride her past every watering can, then home to the plant — it only takes her once she's carrying the lot.
Your team shares 4 silly bands a level, split evenly — with four players that is one each, and your slots glow in your colour.
Lay one however you like: tap both ends, drag one out with a finger, or stretch it between two.
Tap a band to take it back. Anyone can hit PLAY. 🪴
