What the animation is doing

This is a real simulation, not a canned morph: the flexagon's closed band of triangles is folded by rotating its hinges, and at every frame the loop-closure is solved so the band stays a genuine closed loop. Each flex closes the hexagon into a three-bladed star along one set of creases, the layers regroup, and it blooms open along a different set — that regrouping is what exposes a new face. Watch the underside: the face you were just looking at ends up face-down there, which is exactly what a paper flexagon does. Shown with the minimum layers (a trihexaflexagon's band); a hexahexaflexagon flexes its main 1·2·3 cycle exactly like this, with thicker stacks and three more faces hiding deeper.
