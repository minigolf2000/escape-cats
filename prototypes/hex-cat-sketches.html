Hex — Sticker Sketches

Redesign studies for the Hex Clicker cat, working from the holographic sticker reference: white almond eyes with green pupils (today's colors, flipped), a nose-and-mouth mark, curved whiskers, taller ears. Sketch pass only — rigging and animation come next.

Reference

Sticker study · vector redraw

Traced proportions from the photo: tall splayed ears (left a touch higher — kept, it's charming), cheeks widest at eye level, big tilted almond eyes, downcast pupils, features sitting low on the face.

Current in-game Hex

As shipped in hex-cat.svg, the game, the frozen solo build and the reveal lab: green almond eyes, black pupils, w-mouth, six straight whiskers. The sticker flips the eye colors and softens everything else.

Candidates

A · Faithful

The study dropped straight into the game's 200×210 frame. Downcast pupils exactly as printed — sweet, a little aloof.

B · Hybrid

Sticker face on the existing head silhouette. Every animation anchor (ear pivots, eye centers, mouth box) stays where the rig already expects it — the cheapest ship.

C · Faithful, forward gaze

Same geometry as A with pupils at the rig's rest pose. In-game the pupils are live (gaze drift, golden-mouse tracking) — A's downcast look is just this cat glancing at her nose.

Does it survive the rig? · candidate C

Same verbs cat.js already speaks: eyes scaleY to blink and sleep, pupils translate for gaze and scale X for slit→dilate. The one new trick: each pupil is a full circle clipped to its eye, so gaze can push it against the lid (that's what makes the sticker's downcast look) instead of being clamped 4 units from center.

Legibility & palette

At game sizes

The white eyes carry more contrast against the black head than today's green, so she actually reads better small. Whiskers at 3px stroke hold up; the nose disappears below ~40px, which is fine.

Palette & rigging notes

head #111114 keyline #F4F4F2 pupil · sticker #4CA352 pupil · current green #65C256
  • Color flip, same ids. #eyeLeft/Right go #65C256→white, #irisLeft/Right go black→green. The ids survive (again) — cat.js doesn't change a line for color.
  • Pupils get clipPaths. Full green circles clipped to the eye almond; gaze translate can then ride the lid edge (the sticker's downcast look) instead of the current 4-unit clamp.
  • New static #nose; #mouth stays its own path but its top edge moves 147→~156, so the yawn's measured 2.2× ceiling in cat.js gets re-measured (its comment already says to).
  • Whiskers stay one path — six straight lines become four curved strokes; fan/twitch transforms untouched.
  • Ear split carries over. B inherits the game's split ear groups verbatim; A/C need the taller ears re-split (headFill / headOutline / earLeft / earRight) at rigging time.
  • Holo glitter → night phase. The sticker sparkle maps onto the game's starfield: a faint star-speckle inside the silhouette while she dreams. Free thematic win, animation-phase candidate.
