What am I looking at? (design notes)

This is a bird’s-eye playtest rig. In the real game each of these tiles is a separate phone and each player sees only their own screen — here you drive the whole table so we can feel the puzzles fast.

Source — emits the beam
Target — must be hit (matching colour)
Corner — bends the beam 90°
Straight — carries it in a line
Mirror — reflects from any side
Splitter — straight + 90° at once
  • Adjacency = the real hand-off. Here the beam crosses wherever two phones sit edge-to-edge. On real phones that physical alignment is what the “swipe the beam to your neighbour” connect-gesture stands in for — the sim shows the goal state without the gesture friction.
  • Rotation is the core verb. Clicking a phone rotates it 90° (on a real phone you’d turn the device; we snap to 90° so compass noise never matters).
  • What we’re testing: do the puzzles read? Is “rotate + arrange” satisfying, or fiddly? Is the fork (two-player) moment fun? Poke at it and react.
